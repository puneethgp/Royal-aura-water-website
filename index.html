About The Brand

Royal Aura represents the pinnacle of purity and refined taste. Sourced from the pristine historic valleys near Vani Vilas Sagara in Chitradurga, Karnataka, our water undergoes a state-of-the-art 12-stage purification system. We combine advanced Reverse Osmosis, UV Sterilization, and Ozone enrichment to deliver balanced, mineral-rich, crisp hydration crafted for royalty.

Owner Profile

Likith, Founder & Managing Director of Royal Aura Beverages. A visionary entrepreneur dedicated to elevating standard daily hydration into an exquisite wellness experience. With a background in beverage engineering and quality assurance, Likith ensures that every single bottle leaving the plant meets the absolute highest national quality benchmarks.
